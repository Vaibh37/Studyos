How it works

01

Choose a session length

Select a preset or set a custom duration to match the task in front of you. Use shorter sessions for revision or longer sessions for sustained work.

02

Pause and resume

Pause when you need a break and resume when you are ready. Keep the timer in sync with your actual study session.

03

Review your study history

Connect sessions to subjects and use the progress page to see how your study time is distributed across the week.
